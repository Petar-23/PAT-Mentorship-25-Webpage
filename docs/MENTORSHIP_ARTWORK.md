# PAT Mentorship: Bildmotive

Originale, mit dem integrierten Bildgenerator für diese Mentorship-Oberfläche erzeugte Motive. Die Chartbilder sind dekorative Kunstmotive; sie zeigen keine Lehrbeispiele oder Marktprognosen.

Die WebP-Dateien sind auf 1200 Pixel Breite reduziert. Die PNG-Originale bleiben im lokalen Verzeichnis des Bildgenerators erhalten.

## market-focus.webp

Pfad: `public/images/mentorship/market-focus.webp`

Prompt:

Create an original highly art-directed editorial image for PAT, a serious modern price-action education app. Landscape 1536x1024, no UI mockup. In a warm ivory monochrome studio, an oversized beautiful black-rimmed optical loupe rests at a shallow angle on a large sheet of uncoated cream paper bearing an actual candlestick price chart printed in rich black ink: recognisable small rectangular candle bodies, fine vertical wicks, alternating hollow and filled candles, an uneven sideways market structure, no universal upward trend. Through the lens, a few candlesticks are sharply magnified with subtle optical distortion and paper fibres, while the surrounding chart softly falls out of focus. The image is about looking more carefully and understanding context. The visual sensibility of a thoughtful contemporary science magazine or the photographic editorial art on Anthropic's website: tactile, restrained, intelligent, slightly unexpected; NOT finance stock art and NOT luxury product photography. Lots of warm ivory negative space on the LEFT; lens and most of the chart on the RIGHT. Background #faf9f5, graphite #141413, warm paper #e8e6dc. One very small terracotta #d97757 handwritten circle around a candle, no other color. Soft natural directional light and beautiful fine analogue grain. No green, no neon, no computer screen, no trading app, no text, no lettering, no numbers, no labels, no arrows, no glowing elements, no generic 3D icon. Candlestick chart structure must look authentic and quietly sophisticated.

## market-structure.webp

Pfad: `public/images/mentorship/market-structure.webp`

Prompt:

Create an original editorial artwork for PAT, a modern price-action learning app, landscape 1536x1024. Theme: the structure hidden in a candlestick chart. A beautifully photographed physical paper composition: a large off-white uncoated print of an authentic black candlestick chart, with thin vertical wicks and small filled or hollow rectangular bodies, moving through an irregular sequence of rises, consolidation and retracement. Parts of the printed paper are carefully cut and lifted into three shallow folded planes, so the same price chart crosses physical layers and shadows. No green or red up/down coloring: rich charcoal ink and ivory only, with one tiny fine terracotta pencil construction line. It must feel like an intelligent contemporary editorial art commission, restrained, tactile and curious, with the cream and charcoal visual world of Anthropic. Crisp ink edges with microscopic paper grain, subtly imperfect real cut edges, natural window light, delicate believable shadows, sophisticated asymmetric composition and generous empty margins. Close enough to recognise real candlestick charts; no prices, axes, numbers, letters, text, logo, UI, bullish arrows, financial stock-photo cliches, glossy plastics or neon. Quiet warm ivory background #faf9f5; dark ink #141413. A physically crafted artwork photographed with care, not a flat vector or a chart made with HTML.

## exchange.webp

Pfad: `public/images/mentorship/exchange.webp`

Prompt:

Create a refined original editorial macro photograph for PAT, a modern trading education app, as part of a consistent tactile monochrome paper-art identity. Subject: two sculptural folded ribbons made of many layered thin ivory paper sheets, facing one another in a quietly playful almost conversational gesture, connected by a single very fine burnt-sienna thread. There are no faces, no literal chat bubbles and no characters. Thoughtful negative space between the forms. Natural imperfect cut paper edges, exquisite paper strata and tactile grain, deep charcoal inner shadows, restrained analogue photography. Off-white #f8f7f3 seamless surface and backdrop. Soft directional studio light. Monochrome cream, graphite and black; the tiny thread is the only warm accent. Art-school sophistication and intellectual curiosity, not luxury. Landscape 1536x1024 composition, centrally balanced forms with much breathing room. No text, no lettering, no icons, no UI, no green, no candlesticks or trading charts, no money cliches, no shiny plastic, no neon or gradient blobs.
